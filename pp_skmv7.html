1. Introduction

SKMV7 ("the App") is an employee attendance management application designed to help authorized managers and administrators manage employee attendance, register employee faces, perform facial recognition, and record check-in and check-out activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how SKMV7 collects, uses, stores, processes, and shares personal information when you use the App. We handle personal information in accordance with applicable laws. Where required, we obtain appropriate consent or establish another valid legal basis before processing personal information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Employee and Account Information

Depending on the features used, the App may process:

  • Employee names and employee identification numbers.
  • Company codes and organizational information.
  • Account and authentication information.
  • Department and other employee details provided by the organization.
  • Manager and administrator account information.

2.2 Facial Recognition and Biometric Information

SKMV7 may process facial images and facial features to register and identify employees during attendance operations.

This information may include:

  • Facial images captured during face registration or attendance.
  • Facial features or biometric templates, where generated.
  • Face verification and identity matching results.
  • Attendance records associated with an identification result.

Facial information is used for configured employee identification and attendance purposes. Access to this information should be restricted to authorized personnel.

The actual processing and storage arrangements depend on the deployed application. The organization must disclose whether facial images or templates are stored on the device, on a server, or through an authorized service provider.

2.3 Camera Information

Camera permission is used for employee face registration and facial recognition during attendance operations. Camera access is intended for these features and should be used only as necessary for the relevant functionality.

2.4 Location Information

Where location-based attendance is enabled, SKMV7 may collect the device's current GPS location when recording attendance. Location information may be used to:

  • Associate a location with a check-in or check-out.
  • Verify attendance at an authorized workplace.
  • Support attendance validation and administrative reviews.

Location collection depends on the application's configuration and device permissions. Continuous background location tracking should not be assumed unless it is actually implemented.

2.5 Attendance Information

The App may process:

  • Check-in and check-out timestamps.
  • Employee attendance status and history.
  • Attendance reports and verification results.
  • Duplicate attendance detection information.
  • Attendance location details, where enabled.

2.6 Technical and Diagnostic Information

Limited technical information may be processed to authenticate requests, maintain application security, diagnose errors, and support reliable operation.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Register and identify employees.
  • Perform facial recognition and attendance verification.
  • Record employee check-ins and check-outs.
  • Manage group attendance operations.
  • Prevent duplicate or invalid attendance entries.
  • Generate attendance reports for authorized users.
  • Maintain account security and investigate misuse.
  • Troubleshoot and improve application reliability.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. How We Share Information

Personal information may be shared only where necessary for legitimate operational purposes and as permitted by applicable law.

  • Employers and administrators: To manage employees, review attendance, and generate reports.
  • Service providers: With authorized hosting, infrastructure, database, or technical service providers when required to operate the App.
  • Legal authorities: When disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • Business transfers: Where legally permitted in connection with a merger, restructuring, or transfer of business operations.

We do not sell personal or biometric information for advertising purposes.

5. Data Storage and Security

SKMV7 uses its configured backend services and databases to support account management and attendance operations. Information may be stored on company-controlled infrastructure or with an authorized hosting provider.

Reasonable security measures should include:

  • Secure authentication and authorization.
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for network communication.
  • Restricted access to databases and servers.
  • Appropriate safeguards for biometric information.
  • Monitoring for suspected security incidents.
  • Secure deletion when information is no longer required.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable employment, legal, and regulatory requirements.

Retention may vary for:

  • Employee account information.
  • Facial images and biometric templates.
  • Attendance history and reports.
  • Security logs and diagnostic records.

Information should be securely deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required, where appropriate and legally permitted. Some attendance records may need to be retained by the employer to meet legal obligations.

7. App Permissions

Camera Permission

Used for employee face registration and facial recognition during attendance operations.

Location Permission

Used to obtain GPS location for attendance recording and location verification when the feature is enabled.

Internet Permission

Used to authenticate users, communicate with backend services, submit attendance records, and retrieve authorized information.

Users can manage permissions in their device settings. Disabling a required permission may prevent the associated feature from working.

8. Facial Recognition and Biometric Privacy

Facial recognition may involve sensitive biometric information. Organizations using SKMV7 are responsible for ensuring that its deployment complies with applicable privacy, employment, and biometric-data laws.

Where required, employees should be informed about:

  • The purpose of facial recognition.
  • The facial information collected and how it is processed.
  • Where the information is stored.
  • Who can access the information.
  • How long the information is retained.
  • How to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.
  • Available alternative attendance procedures, where required.

Facial recognition can be affected by lighting, camera quality, image angle, and other technical conditions. Organizations should provide a process for reviewing and correcting attendance errors.

9. Children's Privacy

SKMV7 is designed for workplace employee attendance management and is not intended for children. If information is found to have been collected in violation of applicable law, appropriate steps will be taken to address the issue.

10. Third-Party Services

SKMV7 may rely on hosting providers, database services, authentication systems, or other technical services. Where third parties process personal information, their access should be limited to what is necessary and governed by appropriate contractual and security measures.

Third-party services that independently collect information may have their own privacy policies. Relevant service providers should be identified in the App's disclosures where required.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information.

  • Request deletion where legally permitted.

  • Submit a privacy complaint to the appropriate authority.

Employees may need to contact their employer or authorized administrator for attendance-related requests. Requests about the App's handling of personal information may also be directed to SKM Team.
